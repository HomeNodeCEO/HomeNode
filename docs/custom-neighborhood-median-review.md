# Custom neighborhood review clarity

The legacy Sales Sample Representativeness card compares medians, not distributions.
Equal medians can coexist with very different ranges. It must not be described as
proof that every house is similar, nor as the retained workspace's reliability result.

Legacy `neighborhood_age_*` fields have two producers: land-use elapsed ages and
pocket year-built statistics. Saved calendar years are displayed without grouping
commas and with a Year Built label; ordinary elapsed ages keep Age (years). Mixed or
unrecognized ranges are flagged for review. No saved evidence is converted or rewritten.

Calendar years are never denominators for percentage deviation. The review card
shows the absolute median year gap and excludes that measure from its percentage
score. At least three comparable medians are still required. The number of scored
measures and the median-only limitation are explicit. Real elapsed-age, value, PPSF,
and GLA calculations retain their previous formula. A future distribution-based
representativeness measure requires full paired populations and a versioned policy,
not an invented distribution inferred from three range values.

The static range formatter is shared by the editing workspace and printable report;
currency/grouped dimensions and two-decimal PPSF remain intact. A fractional median
year is retained rather than rounded into a falsely exact year.

Pocket exploration precedes Neighborhood Characteristics and its market-area map.
There is still one print-hidden, non-deferred host; no extra capture, save loop, or
report application is introduced. Exploration and accepted report content remain separate.

Study geography is a location troubleshooting/review tool. It checks same-address
CAD parcels and supports a flagged city/ZIP/coordinate override for legacy market
studies. It does not merge parcels, transfer ownership, choose pockets, or alter the
retained workspace's subject/location binding.
